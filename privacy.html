Zyron Bot - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Zyron Bot may collect, use, and protect information when users interact with the bot on Discord.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled by a server, Zyron may process information such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, usernames, moderation events, verification status, and bot configuration settings.

3. Why Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide moderation, security, verification, logging, server-management, and other requested bot features.

4. Data Minimization

Zyron is intended to process only information reasonably required for its enabled functionality. Zyron does not intentionally request passwords, Discord account tokens, or other authentication secrets from users.

5. Data Sharing

Zyron does not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be processed through Discord or third-party services when required for a specific feature or technical operation.

6. Data Retention

Certain configuration or logging information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the relevant server features. Retention may vary depending on the feature and server configuration.

7. Server Administrator Responsibility

Server administrators are responsible for configuring Zyron and deciding which logging, moderation, verification, or security features are enabled on their server.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information processed by Zyron. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Discord

Zyron operates on Discord and is subject to Discord's platform rules and policies. Users should also review Discord's own privacy and security policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Zyron is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children outside the functionality provided through Discord.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Zyron's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the Zyron development or support team through the official Zyron support channels.
