Zyron™ - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Zyron™ may process and store information when users interact with the bot on Discord. Zyron™ is developed by Ashu and operated under Ashu Applications.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the enabled features, Zyron™ may process and store necessary Discord-related information such as User IDs, Server IDs, Channel IDs, Role IDs, usernames, verification status, bot configuration, moderation events, security events, and relevant message content.

3. Message Content

Where required for moderation, security, anti-spam, or related functionality, Zyron™ may process and store relevant message content. Stored message content is retained for up to 7 days.

4. Security and Moderation Logs

Security and moderation logs may include information about Anti-Nuke, Anti-Raid, Anti-Bot, Auto-Moderation, bans, permission changes, whitelist changes, verification activity, and other security events. Such logs are retained for up to 7 days.

5. Why Information Is Used

Information is used only as needed to provide Zyron™ features such as verification, server security, moderation, Auto-Moderation, Anti-Nuke, Anti-Raid, Anti-Bot protection, whitelist management, logging, and related server-management functionality.

6. Data Minimization

Zyron™ is intended to process only information reasonably required for its enabled functionality. Zyron™ does not intentionally request passwords, Discord account tokens, or other authentication secrets from users.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Zyron™ does not intentionally use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics, or visitor-tracking systems on its legal pages.

8. Third-Party Services

Zyron™ does not intentionally use separate third-party services or APIs for its data processing at this time. Zyron™ operates through Discord, which is a third-party platform with its own policies and data practices.

9. Data Sharing

Zyron™ does not sell, rent, or intentionally share stored user or server data with third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes. Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or necessary to comply with valid legal obligations.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Stored message content and security/moderation logs are retained for up to 7 days. When Zyron™ is removed from a Discord server, stored data associated with that server is intended to be deleted immediately.

11. Server Administrator Responsibility

Server administrators are responsible for configuring Zyron™ and deciding which logging, moderation, verification, or security features are enabled on their server. Administrators should ensure that their use of Zyron™ is appropriate for their server and community.

12. Data Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information processed by Zyron™. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security or protection from every possible technical failure or unauthorized access.

13. Discord

Zyron™ operates on Discord and is subject to Discord's applicable platform rules and policies. Users should also review Discord's own privacy and security policies.

14. Age and Platform Requirements

Zyron™ does not impose a separate age requirement. Users must meet the age and eligibility requirements applicable to their use of Discord and its services.

15. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Zyron™ features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The Last Updated date will be changed when this page is revised, and updates may also be announced through official Zyron™ channels when available.
